# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operations engineers, dispatchers, and end users who need real-time tracking, routing, geofencing, and map visualization for transportation, delivery, and marker-based exploration.

## Product Purpose

To provide an enterprise-grade interactive map platform featuring high-fidelity performance, offline capability, 3D rendering, live driver tracking via WebSockets, and spatial pricing.

## Brand Personality

Sleek, Precise, Reliable. Visually, it should prioritize high contrast, clarity, readability, and clean typography. Explicitly avoid unnecessary visual clutter or gimmicks.

## Anti-references

- SaaS template clichés (e.g., the hero-metric template with tiny tracked all-caps eyebrows, arbitrary numbering tags like 01 / 02 / 03).
- Glassmorphism (explicitly forbidden by the user).
- Decorative side-stripe borders or gradient text.
- Over-animated elements or hovering image transforms.

## Design Principles

1. **Serve the Data First**: The maps and telemetry are the primary focus. Styling must enhance readability, not distract.
2. **Predictable Interaction**: Workflows must feel instantaneous and robust (low latency, optimistic UI, clear loading states).
3. **Impeccable Hierarchy**: Use font-weight and layout spacing to establish clear reading priorities rather than loud colors or borders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 for body and placeholder text.
- Full support for prefers-reduced-motion (animations strictly degradation-friendly or crossfade-based).
- Keyboard-navigable dialogs and controls.
